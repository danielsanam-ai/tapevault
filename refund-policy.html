In short: if you request a refund within 7 days of payment and have not activated your license, we refund the full amount. TapeVault has no automatic renewal, so you are never charged again unless you complete a new checkout.

1. Scope

This Refund Policy applies to paid TapeVault subscriptions purchased through tapevault.net. Payments are processed by Toss Payments Co., Ltd. ("Toss Payments"), our payment service provider. This policy supplements our Terms of Service.

2. Free trial and beta access

Free trial and beta access are provided at no charge, so there is nothing to refund. We never charge your card at the end of a trial or beta period.

TapeVault has no automatic renewal. A payment is made only when you complete a new, explicit checkout.

3. Full refund within 7 days

You may request a refund within 7 days of the payment date. If your license has not been activated, we will refund the full amount you paid, including VAT.

The 7 days are counted from the day the payment was completed.

4. After activation

Once a license has been activated, the paid subscription is treated as used and we do not refund the used subscription period, except as described in Sections 5 and 6 or where mandatory law requires otherwise. You can stop using TapeVault at any time, and because there is no automatic renewal, no further charges will occur.

5. Defective or misdescribed software

If the Software materially differs from what was described on our website, or does not work as described and we are unable to fix the problem, you may request cancellation and a full refund within 3 months of receiving the Software and within 30 days of becoming aware of the problem.

6. Duplicate or erroneous charges

If you were charged twice for the same purchase, or charged an incorrect amount, we will refund the duplicate payment or the difference in full, regardless of license activation.

7. How to request a refund

Email support@tapevault.net and include:

  • The email address used for the purchase
  • The payment date and amount
  • The reason for your request (optional, but it helps us improve)

We aim to reply within 3 business days.

8. Refund method and timing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method by cancelling the payment through Toss Payments. We submit the cancellation within 3 business days of approval.

How long it takes for the refund to appear on your card statement depends on your card issuer and is outside our control; it often takes several business days.

9. Statutory rights and Enterprise plans

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under applicable consumer protection law, including Korea's Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce and any mandatory rights in your country of residence.

Enterprise plans are governed by the terms of your signed agreement, which prevail over this policy where they differ.
