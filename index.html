How it works
Simple three-step workflow

TapeVault turns complex LTO tape operations into a straightforward desktop workflow.

01
Connect your LTO drive
Plug in your LTO drive and insert a tape. TapeVault detects hardware instantly and shows drive status and capacity.
02
Organize your archive
Create projects, drag files into the queue, assign to tapes. Metadata and checksums managed automatically.
03
Archive & retrieve
Write to tape with one click. Search and retrieve any file across your entire tape library in seconds.

FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Which LTO drive generations does TapeVault support?
TapeVault supports LTO-4 through LTO-9. Single-drive in all plans; multi-drive in Pro and Enterprise.
Does TapeVault require an internet connection?
No. TapeVault is fully offline-capable after initial license activation. Ideal for air-gapped environments.
What file formats are supported?
Any file your OS can read — MP4, MOV, MXF, AVI, PDF, ZIP, JPG, PNG and more. Video files get automatic thumbnail generation.
How does the free trial work?
Download and use TapeVault fully featured for 30 days — no credit card required.
Is there an Enterprise deployment option?
Yes. Enterprise includes on-site deployment, dedicated SLA, and custom onboarding. Contact sales for details.
